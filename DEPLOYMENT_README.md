## ⚡ 5-Minute Deployment (Recommended)

### Option 1: Netlify (Easiest)
1. Go to [netlify.com](https://netlify.com) → Sign up free
2. **Drag the entire `/dist` folder** to the deploy area
3. Your site goes live instantly at `random-name.netlify.app`
4. Buy domain at [namecheap.com](https://namecheap.com) ($10-15/year)
5. In Netlify: Site Settings → Domain Management → Add custom domain
6. Update your domain's DNS to Netlify's nameservers
7. **Done!** Your keto site is live on your custom domain

### Option 2: Vercel (Developer-Friendly)
1. Go to [vercel.com](https://vercel.com) → Sign up free
2. Click "Add New" → Upload `/dist` folder
3. Site deploys automatically
4. Add custom domain in Project Settings
5. Update DNS records as instructed
